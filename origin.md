The abdominal experiment used a single-shot FSE readout during breath-holding, and the FFA designs imposed a minimum refocusing angle of 30° (Sections 3.1 and 3.4.3). These measures reduce exposure to respiratory motion and very low-angle signal pathways. In particular, the 3D ASL acquisition lasted approximately 10 minutes and showed no obvious motion-related degradation on visual inspection.

The present work addresses the joint optimization of VFA schedules and k-space filters for SNR under prescribed PSF constraints. Motion sensitivity is a recognized consideration for low-refocusing-angle VFA readouts.
